This book is a collective of a lot of parts that go into making a software. I am writing this as a story of building an app, but this format hopefully can be applied to any other software project as well. The format is gonna be similar, except maybe for the first few chapters where the chapters are linear. Then we are going to branch out into serveral things.

I will explain this with an allegory to building a house. There are multitude of things going and they go in parallel. You can think this book does something similar. You have masonry, plumbing, electricity related stuff etc going mostly parallel. Now one can stick to what they like to learn, but suppose this book was about building a house, it would have separate chapters at each step about each of the things going when lets say building the living room, the bedroom or the bathroom.

This can be illustrated as below:
- Living Room
	- Masonry
	- Plumbing
	- Electrical fittings
- Bedroom
	- Masonry
	- Plumbing
	- Electrical fittings
- Bathroom
	- Masonry
	- Plumbing
	- Electrical fittings

You see how we made chapters for everything. Similarly in software that we write these days there are several parts, the product design, the design files, the code which can be further divided into backend, frontend and infrastructure (CI/CD). 

The book is always going to be ongoing effort because things will change inevitably and my plan is to keep as many of the categories up to date. This book should let you choose what you want to know and skip the rest (although you are welcome to learn everything as well). For example if you want to learn app development, a bit of product plan, the basics of design (without going to the nitty gritties) and the actual code implementation on the mobile app is what you want to go through.

On the other hand if you want to work on the backend (which is still a long way in the future from the moment I am writing this), you can go through the product, backend and maybe continuous integration and deployment. 

The fundamental is you can choose to look at what went into making the app either as a big picture or in detail for the topics of your choosing. I am writing the entire thing at the moment and I love to know every part of the process but I leave what you want to learn up to you.
