I have been meaning to write a book and share what I know for a very very long time. And I finally found the drive to do it. This book is the first thing I plan to write and publish and I want to do it on a very short time period. Even though I have a full time job, I wish to complete and publish this book real soon.

But even though I am trying to push myself to complete this book at the fastest pace I can with all the procastination, I hope I can get it done in a meaningfully small amount of time without compromising on either quality or content. This foreword is to wish me luck in my endeavour.

## Who is this book for

### Me

This book is first and foremost for **me**. This is a selfish mission to attain quite a few things.
- I want to push myself to do something meaningful
- I want to build a book unlike other
- I want to showcase myself and my talents. 
- I want to prove a point

Out of all this, I really want to show off. I have been way to laid back to show what I am capable of and I am pouring a lot of effort to show I am more than what my education or career convey. 

### For people who want to learn more than the basics

I have a lot of complaints as a person with quite a bit of experience in my field. Learning from books becomes hard after a point. Every book is essentially the same. You choose a topic, start from the basics. Learn a skill with the bare minimum examples. 

This books intends to change that. I am willing to write a process rather than a recipe. The recipe has a definite start and end, but this book as a process will meander around and cover building something and continuously iterating over it. This book has virtually no end and will go on as long my enthusiasm doesn't run dry and/or till there are people to pick up the pieces and work on it.

I would say read the introduction to understand why this book, and __how__ this book.
